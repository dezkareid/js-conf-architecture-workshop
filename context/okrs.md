# Mentory corp: OKRs

## FY2027

### Objective 1: Launch the Mentoring platform by Q1 2027

*Supports: Mission — enable the mentees to find mentors to reach their goals. Nothing in
the mission is served until mentors and mentees can actually meet on the platform.*

**Key Results:**

- Achieve 10,000 registered users by Q1 2027.
- Achieve 1,000 active users by Q1 2027.
- Achieve 10,000 mentorías (completed mentoring sessions) by Q1 2027.
